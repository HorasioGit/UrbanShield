# 🎬 UrbanShield — Video Teaser Production Plan
**Format:** Landscape 16:9 | **Durasi:** 30 detik | **Bahasa:** Indonesia  
**Narasi:** Teks Overlay + Musik | **Visual:** AI-generated + Mockup Dashboard

---

## 🎯 Creative Brief

| Elemen | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Produk** | UrbanShield — Flood Risk Intelligence untuk Logistik Urban |
| **Target Audience** | Juri Microsoft Elevate, investor, perusahaan logistik |
| **Tone** | Cinematic · Tense · Futuristic · Confident |
| **Tagline** | *"Ketika Banjir Ancam Jakarta, UrbanShield Tahu Jalannya."* |

## 🎞️ Storyboard — Scene by Scene

### 🔴 ACT 1: THE PROBLEM (0–8 detik)

**Misi:** Bangkitkan urgensi dan empati. Penonton harus merasa "ini masalah nyata."

---

**SCENE 1 — 0s hingga 3s** — *Pembuka sinematik. Langit gelap, hujan deras.*

**Visual:** Drone shot turun perlahan ke jalan raya Jakarta yang tergenang banjir. Lampu kendaraan menciptakan refleksi cahaya di air. Beberapa truk besar terlihat berhenti di tengah jalan.

**Teks Overlay:**
```
JAKARTA
Setiap tahun. Setiap musim hujan.
```
*Font: monospace, muncul dari kiri satu per satu, warna putih*

**Veo 3 Prompt:**
```
Cinematic aerial drone shot slowly descending over a flooded Jakarta urban highway at night during
heavy rain. Large logistics trucks stranded in floodwater. Headlights creating neon reflections on
the water surface. Dark moody atmosphere, city lights blurring through rain. Hyperrealistic, 4K,
cinematic color grading, shallow depth of field. 16:9 landscape.
```

---

**SCENE 2 — 3s hingga 8s** — *Close up masalah. Kemacetan dan kerugian.*

**Visual:** Close-up wajah supir truk yang frustrasi melihat jalan tergenang. Cut to: angka kerugian muncul melayang seperti data hologram.

**Teks Overlay:**
```
Rp 2.500.000 / pengiriman
Waktu terbuang. Biaya membengkak.
Tanpa solusi.
```
*Font: bold merah-oranye, efek glitch/flicker sesaat*

**Veo 3 Prompt:**
```
Close-up cinematic shot of a frustrated truck driver looking through a rain-drenched windshield
at a flooded road. Then cut to a dark logistics warehouse with worried workers. Dramatic low-key
lighting. Tense atmosphere. Hyperrealistic film style, 16:9 landscape, cinematic lens flare.
```

---

### 🔵 ACT 2: THE TECHNOLOGY (8s hingga 22s)

**Misi:** Perlihatkan UrbanShield beraksi. Terasa canggih, real-time, dan andal.

---

**SCENE 3 — 8s hingga 13s** — *Transisi dramatis. Masalah bertransformasi menjadi solusi.*

**Visual:** Layar gelap. Dari kegelapan, garis-garis data muncul seperti sirkuit yang menyala — lalu perlahan membentuk tampilan dashboard UrbanShield. Mockup UI peta Jakarta muncul, rute-rute bercahaya cyan terbentuk.

**Teks Overlay:**
```
UrbanShield
Real-time Flood Risk Intelligence
```
*Logo UrbanShield muncul dengan efek glow perlahan, warna cyan*

**Veo 3 Prompt:**
```
Dramatic tech reveal animation. Dark screen with glowing cyan circuit lines emerging and forming
a futuristic map interface of Jakarta. Data points and route lines light up in cyan and blue neon.
HUD-style overlay elements appear. Futuristic command center aesthetic. Motion graphics, 16:9
landscape, sci-fi dashboard reveal, deep navy background.
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SCENE 3b — MOCKUP INSERT (11s–14s):** Gunakan screenshot statis dari dashboard UrbanShield (Route Simulator) — peta Jakarta dengan rute berwarna + Probability Matrix. Ini bagian paling autentik dan kuat untuk juri!

---

**SCENE 4 — 14s hingga 18s** — *Fitur unggulan diperlihatkan satu per satu.*

**Visual:** Cut cepat bergantian: Panel "Live (API)" menyala → Angka XGBoost berputar → Gen-AI Advisory muncul teks rekomendasi.

**Teks Overlay (bergantian):**
```
📡 Data Cuaca Real-Time — Open-Meteo API
🤖 Prediksi Banjir — Model XGBoost
💡 Rekomendasi Rute — Generative AI
```
*Setiap teks slide masuk, warna cyan, durasi 1.3 detik each*

**Veo 3 Prompt:**
```
Fast-paced futuristic UI montage. Multiple glowing dashboard screens showing live weather data,
probability matrices with percentages, and AI text recommendations. Screens have holographic
neon cyan glow effect. Data flows and charts animate rapidly. Dark navy background, cyberpunk
aesthetic, 16:9 landscape, energetic motion.
```

---

**SCENE 5 — 18s hingga 22s** — *The Result — rute aman ditemukan.*

**Visual:** Animasi dari atas: peta Jakarta, rute merah (berbahaya) berganti menjadi rute hijau/cyan. Truk logistik bergerak lancar di jalan yang bebas banjir.

**Teks Overlay:**
```
Rute Aman Ditemukan. ⚡
Net Savings: Rp 2.500.000
```
*Angka muncul dengan animasi counting up, warna hijau emerald*

**Veo 3 Prompt:**
```
Aerial timelapse of Jakarta streets at dawn after rain. Logistics trucks moving smoothly on clear
roads. Overlaid with glowing green route lines on a map. Hopeful and confident mood, warm sunrise
light mixing with cool tech blue overlays. Cinematic resolution, 16:9 landscape.
```

---

### ✅ ACT 3: BRAND CLOSER (22s hingga 30s)

**Misi:** Tinggalkan kesan kuat. Nama produk dan ekosistem teknologinya diingat.

---

**SCENE 6 — 22s hingga 27s** — *The Promise.*

**Visual:** Aerial Jakarta dari sudut yang indah — langit mulai cerah setelah hujan. Data stream overlay mengalir di atas kota (biru/cyan elegan).

**Teks Overlay:**
```
Logistik lebih cerdas.
Kota lebih tangguh.
```
*Teks besar bold, muncul perlahan dengan fade, warna putih*

**Veo 3 Prompt:**
```
Breathtaking cinematic aerial shot of Jakarta skyline after rain, golden hour lighting mixed with
dramatic storm clouds clearing. City viewed from high altitude, rivers and roads visible below.
Subtle cyan data stream overlay effects. Inspiring, hopeful, powerful. 4K hyperrealistic, 16:9
landscape, epic wide angle.
```

---

**SCENE 7 — 27s hingga 30s** — *Logo Closer.*

**Visual:** Layar hitam bersih. Logo UrbanShield muncul di tengah dengan efek glow cyan. Badge teknologi muncul di bawahnya.

**Teks Overlay:**
```
🛡️  UrbanShield
Logistics OS 2.0

⚡ Powered by Microsoft Azure
```
*Semua muncul dengan efek subtle pulse/breathe, cyan + putih*

**Veo 3 Prompt:**
```
Minimalist dark background with a glowing shield logo appearing in the center, surrounded by
subtle particle and light effects in cyan and blue. Corporate tech logo reveal, premium brand feel.
Slow elegant animation. 16:9 landscape, dark navy background, neon glow aesthetic.
```

## 🎵 Arahan Musik

| Segmen | Durasi | Mood | Referensi Genre |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACT 1 (Problem) | 0–8s | Tension, dark, hujan | Cinematic suspense / dark ambient |
| ACT 2 (Tech) | 8–22s | Build-up, energetic, futuristic | Electronic / tech pulse / rising orchestral |
| ACT 3 (Closer) | 22–30s | Resolusi, confident, epic | Uplifting orchestral + electronic hybrid |

**Rekomendasi Sumber Musik Bebas Royalti:**
- [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/) — filter "cinematic tech"
- [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/) — filter "dramatic / futuristic"
- [Epidemic Sound](https://www.epidemicsound.com/) — mood "technology"

> [!TIP]
> Cari 1 track yang punya arc emosional lengkap (tension → build → resolve) agar tidak perlu edit/sambung 2 lagu berbeda. Durasi 30 detik sangat cocok untuk bagian *drop* atau *chorus* sebuah track instrumental.

## 📋 Workflow Produksi

**FASE 1 — PERSIAPAN**
- `[ ]` Finalisasi storyboard ✅
- `[ ]` Ambil screenshot dashboard UrbanShield (5–6 frame terbaik)
- `[ ]` Download musik latar

**FASE 2 — GENERATE AI FOOTAGE (Veo 3 via AI Studio)**
- `[ ]` Scene 1: Banjir Jakarta aerial
- `[ ]` Scene 2: Supir frustrasi + gudang
- `[ ]` Scene 3: Tech reveal / circuit animation
- `[ ]` Scene 5: Rute aman + truk lancar
- `[ ]` Scene 6: Jakarta skyline cerah
- `[ ]` Scene 7: Logo reveal

**FASE 3 — DASHBOARD MOCKUP**
- `[ ]` Screenshot Route Simulator (rute + peta terisi)
- `[ ]` Screenshot Probability Matrix (angka menyala)
- `[ ]` Screenshot Gen-AI Advisory (teks rekomendasi muncul)

**FASE 4 — EDITING (CapCut / DaVinci Resolve)**
- `[ ]` Susun klip sesuai storyboard
- `[ ]` Tambahkan teks overlay (font monospace/futuristic)
- `[ ]` Sinkronkan teks dengan beat musik
- `[ ]` Color grading (dark + cyan tint)
- `[ ]` Transisi (cut cepat + glitch transition)
- `[ ]` Export `1920×1080` (16:9) H.264

**FASE 5 — REVIEW AKHIR**
- `[ ]` Durasi ≤ 30 detik
- `[ ]` Teks overlay terbaca jelas
- `[ ]` Konsistensi warna brand (cyan, navy, putih)
- `[ ]` Nama Azure terlihat di frame terakhir
